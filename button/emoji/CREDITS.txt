To add another emoji, find its code (for example 1f437 for the pig face) and
download https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/jdecked/twemoji@15.1.0/assets/svg/<code>.svg
into this folder. Then add it to MODES in ../index.html and to FILES in ../sw.js.
