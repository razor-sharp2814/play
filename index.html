Good to know

Getting a game on your home screen

Once a game is saved, open it from its own icon. It runs full screen, like an app, and works without signal or Wi-Fi.

Android

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then Add to home screen, then Install.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (on newer versions, tap the three dots first).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Laptop or computer

  1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, then Install. On a Mac using Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock.
Keeping little hands inside the game

Phones can lock themselves to one app, so little fingers can't swipe out of the game.

Android

Switch on App pinning in Settings (Security & privacy, then More security & privacy), open the game, then pin it from the recent apps screen.

To leave, swipe up and hold, then enter your PIN or fingerprint.

iPhone and iPad

Switch on Guided Access in Settings (Accessibility, then Guided Access) and set a passcode. Open the game, triple-click the side button (or the Home button), then tap Start.

To leave, triple-click the same button, enter your passcode, then tap End.
