Good to know

Putting a phone game on your home screen
  1. Open the game in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots), then Add to home screen, then Install.
  3. Open it from the new icon. It runs full screen and works without signal.
Keeping little hands inside the game

Android can lock a phone to one app. Switch on App pinning in Settings (Security & privacy, then More security & privacy), open the game, then pin it from the recent apps screen.

To leave, swipe up and hold, then enter your PIN or fingerprint.
